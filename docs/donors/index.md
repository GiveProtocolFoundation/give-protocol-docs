## How Donations Work

### Card (Fiat) Donations
1. Find a verified charity on Give Protocol.
2. Click **Give Once** or **Give Monthly**.
3. Enter your amount, name, and email.
4. Complete payment in the secure Helcim window (USD) or PayPal (other currencies).
5. Receive a receipt by email.

No crypto wallet or blockchain knowledge required.

### Crypto Donations
1. Connect a crypto wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, or others).
2. Select a token and amount.
3. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.
4. The donation is recorded on the blockchain and visible in your dashboard.

See the [Crypto Giving Guide](/docs/donors/crypto-giving/) for a full walkthrough, including setting up a wallet from scratch.

## Why Donate Through Give Protocol?

### Transparency
Every crypto donation is recorded on a public blockchain, creating a verifiable record of your gift. Card donations are tracked in your dashboard with payment processor references.

### Lower Fees
Give Protocol does not charge platform fees on crypto donations beyond the blockchain's gas fee (typically less than $0.01 on Base or Optimism). Card donations incur standard payment processor fees — you can optionally cover these so 100% of your intended amount reaches the charity.

### Verified Organizations
Charities on Give Protocol are sourced from official public registries and verified through an identity confirmation process. Look for the "Verified nonprofit" badge on charity profiles.

### Donation Records
Access your full donation history, export records as CSV, and download receipts — all from your [Give Dashboard](/docs/donors/tracking-impact/).
